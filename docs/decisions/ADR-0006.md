# ADR-0006: Separate Plymouth and initramfs

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-13

## Context

Loader appearance and OS splash have different consumers and recovery paths.

## Decision

Manage Plymouth separately, with dracut and mkinitcpio internal task adapters.

## Rationale

Theme selection can be tested independently and generator edits remain minimal.

## Alternatives Considered

Couple splash to bootloader or own complete generator configuration.

## Consequences

Conditional rebuilds and two generator-specific recovery tests are required.
